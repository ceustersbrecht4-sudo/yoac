    • other — the referee and touch judges (a shirt colour clearly different from both teams). Hidden from the video by default; tap it to show them.
    • Every other player is put in one of the two teams, even when it's a close call, so a far-away player can occasionally land in the wrong team.
    • unsure — only for players whose shirt was never visible clearly enough to read; left out of the coach report.
    • Numbers are detections across all frames, not number of players.
    • People on the sideline (stewards, touch judges) can still end up in a team.
    • A player leaving the picture and coming back usually gets a new number.
