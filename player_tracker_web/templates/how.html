02 Processing

What happens to the file on the way through

The same stages the upload page shows while it works, with the settings they run on. A 10-second clip takes a few minutes on a laptop. You can close the page once the upload is done.

{% set live = follow or active %} {% if live %}
Your video "{{ live.name }}" is being analysed{% if follow %}. You'll be taken to the tracked video when it's done.{% endif %} Checking...
{% endif %}
  1. Upload

    Send the file

    Straight from your phone's gallery or your laptop.

    limit 2 GB, 2 h
    picture up to 4096 px

  2. Check

    Is it a match?

    Anything that isn't match or training footage is refused and deleted, and its minutes are given back.

    samples 16 frames
    grass in 40% of them
    later: 3+ people in 30% of frames

  3. Shrink

    Make it smaller

    The detector only looks at 1280 pixels across, so the rest is thrown away first. The original is deleted.

    max 1280 px long side
    max 30 fps, no sound

  4. Track

    Find every player

    Each frame is scanned for people, and each person keeps one number from frame to frame, also when they disappear behind others for a moment.

    YOLOv8m at 1280 px
    boxes under 35% sure are dropped
    a hidden player keeps their number for 90 frames (3 s at 30 fps)

  5. Teams

    Sort by shirt

    Shirt pixels from every frame a player is seen in vote on their team, so shade and floodlight don't flip them. A small group in a clearly different colour is the referee and touch judges.

    votes: every frame of a track
    officials: under 15% of players, 65% of their votes

  6. Draw

    Draw the video

    Every player gets a box and a number in their team's real kit colour. Officials aren't drawn.

    H.264, plays in any browser
    download it for the team meeting
