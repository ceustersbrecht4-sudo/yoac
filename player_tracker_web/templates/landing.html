How it works

From phone video to coach report

No tagging, no setup. Just the footage you already film.

  1. 1. Upload

    Pick a match video from your phone or laptop. Wide shots of the pitch work best.

  2. 2. Track

    Every player is found and followed frame by frame, then sorted into the two teams by shirt colour.

  3. 3. Review

    Watch the tracked video and read the coach report, with each moment one tap away.

Coach report

Pointers, not just pictures

YOAC measures line gaps, dog-legs, numbers in the line and players at the breakdown, in attack or defence. Every point links to the moments to review, with the problem circled in red.
