How it works

From phone video to coach report

No tagging, no setup. Just the footage you already film.

  1. 1. Upload

    Pick a match video from your phone or laptop. Wide shots of the pitch work best.

  2. 2. Track

    Every player is found and followed frame by frame, then sorted into teams by shirt colour.

  3. 3. Review

    Watch the tracked video and read the coach report, with each moment one tap away.

What you get

Everything a coach needs from the video

Tracked video

Your footage back with every player boxed and numbered. Download it or watch it on your phone.

Teams by colour

Players are grouped by shirt colour automatically. Hide referees or anyone else with one tap.

Coach report

Line structure, spacing and ruck numbers in attack or defence, with the moments to review.

Ready when your footage is.
