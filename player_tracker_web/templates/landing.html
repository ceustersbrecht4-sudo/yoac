02 Processing

What happens to the file on the way through

The same stages the upload page shows while it works, with the settings they run on. A 10-second clip takes a few minutes on a laptop. You can close the page once the upload is done.

  1. Upload

    Send the file

    Straight from your phone's gallery or your laptop.

    limit 2 GB, 2 h
    picture up to 4096 px

  2. Check

    Is it a match?

    Anything that isn't match or training footage is refused and deleted, and its minutes are given back.

    samples 16 frames
    grass in 40% of them
    later: 3+ people in 30% of frames

  3. Shrink

    Make it smaller

    The detector only looks at 1280 pixels across, so the rest is thrown away first. The original is deleted.

    max 1280 px long side
    max 30 fps, no sound

  4. Track

    Find every player

    Each frame is scanned for people, and each person keeps one number from frame to frame, also when they disappear behind others for a moment.

    YOLOv8m at 1280 px
    boxes under 35% sure are dropped
    a hidden player keeps their number for 90 frames (3 s at 30 fps)

  5. Teams

    Sort by shirt

    Shirt pixels from every frame a player is seen in vote on their team, so shade and floodlight don't flip them. A small group in a clearly different colour is the referee and touch judges.

    votes: every frame of a track
    officials: under 15% of players, 65% of their votes

  6. Draw

    Draw the video

    Every player gets a box and a number in their team's real kit colour. Officials aren't drawn.

    H.264, plays in any browser
    download it for the team meeting

04 Coach report

What the report flags, and the rule behind each flag

Pick a team and whether they were attacking or defending. Each flag comes with the moments it happened, a still with the problem marked in red, and a click takes the video there.

Without marking the pitch, distances are in body lengths: the height of a typical player in that frame.

work on

Holes in the defensive line

Two neighbouring defenders much further apart than they usually stand.

gap > 2.2 x usual spacing, and > usual + 1.5 body lengths
work on

Players out of line (dog-legs)

One defender standing well in front of or behind the rest of the line.

> 2.5 x the usual step out of line, at least 1.5 body lengths
work on

Outnumbered in defence / overlap chances

One side has more players standing in the line than the other.

2 or more extra players in the line
work on

Committing too many to the breakdown

Players who join the ruck aren't standing in the line.

defence: 3+ per ruck on average / attack: 4+
timed

Ruck speed

From the players meeting at the tackle until the group breaks up, for every ruck the camera sees start to end. Scrums are counted too.

quick ball < 3 s / slow ball > 6 s / scrum = 10+ players in one group
going well

The same checks, the other way round

A line that stayed connected, kept its shape or matched the attack's numbers is reported as going well.

These are measurements from one camera: a list of moments to rewatch, not a verdict. Check each one on the video.

Mark the pitch once, get metres

On a wide shot, click at least 4 spots where two pitch lines cross, like the 22 and the 5 m line. The app then follows the camera as it pans and zooms, and the report adds:

Line speed in the first 1.5 s after the ball leaves a ruck: under 1.5 m/s is flagged as passive, 2.5 m/s and more as good.
Mauls, told apart from rucks: a breakdown that moves 3 m or more.

Following the camera runs once per video, a few minutes for a full match.

Questions coaches ask

What footage works best?

A wide view from the stand or a high spot that shows most of the pitch. Phone footage is fine. Close-ups and replays are skipped in the report because team shape can't be judged from them.

Does it recognise players' faces or names?

No. Players are detected as "a person", given a temporary number and grouped by shirt colour. Nobody is identified.

Why does a player get a new number halfway through?

A player who is out of sight for more than 90 frames (3 seconds at 30 fps), for example off the edge of the picture, is picked up as someone new. Their team stays right, because that's decided by shirt colour.

Who can see my videos?

Only you, and the owner of the app for moderation. Videos are deleted automatically after the period of your plan, or earlier when you delete them. See the privacy policy.

How accurate is it?

It's a guide to which moments to review, not a referee. Far-away players can be missed or land in the wrong team, and without marking the pitch, distances are in body lengths. Always check a moment on the video before acting on it.

Can I upload videos of youth teams?

Yes, if your club's rules allow it and players and parents know matches are filmed and analysed. You confirm this with each upload.
