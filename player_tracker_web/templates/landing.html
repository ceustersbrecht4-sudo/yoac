How it works

Three steps, no tagging

  1. Upload the video

    Any match or training video from a phone or camera. A wide shot from the stand works best. Start with a short clip to see how it goes.

  2. Let it run

    The analysis runs on your computer. A 10-second clip takes a few minutes; you can follow the frames it's working on and close the page in the meantime.

  3. Review with the team

    Watch the tracked video, open the report, and jump to each moment. Download the video to show it in the next session.

Measure in metres

Mark the pitch once, get real distances

Click four places where pitch lines cross, such as the 22 and the touchline. From then on the app follows the camera as it pans and zooms, and turns positions into metres: ruck distances, mauls that move, and how fast the defensive line comes up.

Without marking, you still get everything else, measured in body lengths.

FAQ

Questions coaches ask

What footage works best?

A wide view from the stand or a high position that shows most of the pitch. Phone footage is fine. Close-ups and replays are skipped in the report because team shape can't be judged from them.

Does it recognise players' faces or names?

No. Players are detected as "a person", given a temporary number and grouped by shirt colour. Nobody is identified.

Who can see my videos?

Only you, and the owner of the app for moderation. Videos are deleted automatically after the period of your plan, or earlier when you delete them. See the privacy policy.

How accurate is it?

It's a guide to which moments to review, not a referee. Far-away players can be missed or land in the wrong team, and without marking the pitch, distances are in body lengths. Always check a moment on the video before acting on it.

Can I upload videos of youth teams?

Yes, if your club's rules allow it and players and parents know matches are filmed and analysed. You confirm this with each upload.

What happens with other videos?

Uploads are checked automatically. Anything that isn't match or training footage is refused and deleted straight away.
