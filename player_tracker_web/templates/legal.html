{% if doc == 'privacy' %}

This policy explains what personal data YOAC Player Tracker handles, why, and what your rights are. It follows the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

1. Who is responsible

The controller of your personal data is {{ v('owner_name') }}, {{ v('address') }}. Questions or requests: {{ v('email') }}.

2. How this app works

YOAC Player Tracker runs on the owner's own computer. It is not hosted on an internet server, and nothing you upload is sent to anyone else: there are no analytics, no advertising, no tracking services, and the fonts and logos are served by the app itself. You reach it through the local Wi-Fi network.

3. What data we process, and why

Your account

  • What: your username, your password (stored only as a salted hash, never readable), when you created the account and when you accepted the terms.
  • Why: to let you log in and to protect the videos from people without an account.
  • Legal basis: providing the service you signed up for (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)).

Match videos and results

  • What: the videos you upload and what the app makes from them: the tracked video, the position and shirt colour of each person in each frame, and coach reports. Videos show players, referees and sometimes spectators who can be recognised, so they are personal data. Many players may be children.
  • How: people are detected as "a person", followed with a temporary number and grouped by shirt colour. The app does not recognise faces or identify who anyone is, and does not process biometric data to identify people.
  • Why: only to give coaches the tracking and report they asked for. Videos are not used for anything else.
  • Who sees them: everyone with an account on this app can open the analysed videos and reports, and a still frame of the latest analysed video is shown on the front page after logging in.
  • Legal basis: the person who uploads a video must have a lawful basis to use it, usually the club's legitimate interest in coaching (Art. 6(1)(f)) or the consent of the players, and of the parents for players under 16. See the terms of use.

Technical data

  • Your device's network address (IP address) is used to answer your requests and to block repeated wrong passwords. It is kept in memory for at most 5 minutes and is not stored.
  • A few essential cookies keep you logged in. See the cookie policy.

4. Where it is stored and for how long

  • Everything is stored only on the computer that runs the app.
  • Videos and their results are deleted automatically {% if info.video_retention_days %}{{ info.video_retention_days }} days after they were analysed{% else %}when you delete them{% endif %}, or earlier when you press Delete video.
  • Your account is kept until you delete it (see below).
  • We don't share your data with anyone and nothing is transferred outside the EU.

5. Security

Accounts can only be created with a single-use invite code from the owner. Passwords are hashed, must meet minimum rules and are checked against known data breaches: only the first 5 characters of a SHA-1 fingerprint of the password are sent to the Have I Been Pwned service (api.pwnedpasswords.com), never the password itself, and nothing about you. Two-step login with an authenticator app is available for every account. Sessions are signed HttpOnly cookies, forms are protected against cross-site requests, a Content Security Policy blocks injected scripts, and repeated wrong passwords or codes are blocked per device and per account. The connection between your phone or browser and the laptop uses plain HTTP inside your own network and is not encrypted, so only use the app on a Wi-Fi network you trust (not public Wi-Fi).

6. Children

Accounts are for coaches and staff aged 16 or older. Match videos often show players under 16: only upload them if your club's rules allow it and the parents have agreed, and tell players and parents that videos are analysed.

7. Your rights

You have the right to access, correct and delete your data, to restrict or object to its use, to receive it in a portable format, and to withdraw consent at any time. You can:

  • delete any video and its results with the Delete video button on its result page;
  • delete your account below;
  • ask for anything else at {{ v('email') }}. We answer within one month.

If you think your data is not handled properly, you can complain to the Belgian Data Protection Authority (Gegevensbeschermingsautoriteit / Autorité de protection des données), Drukpersstraat 35, 1000 Brussels, www.dataprotectionauthority.be.

These terms apply when you use YOAC Player Tracker, provided by {{ v('owner_name') }}. By creating an account you accept them.

1. Your account

  • You must be 16 or older.
  • Keep your password to yourself. You are responsible for what happens with your account.

2. Videos you upload

  • Only upload videos you are allowed to use: you filmed them yourself or have permission, and using them for coaching analysis fits your club's rules.
  • Make sure players (and parents of players under 16) know that match videos are filmed and analysed, and respect anyone who objects.
  • Don't upload anything unlawful, or videos of people in private situations.
  • The videos stay yours. You only allow the app to process them to give you the analysis.

3. Acceptable use

  • Use the app for sports coaching only, not to monitor or follow specific people outside sport.
  • Don't try to get around the login, reach other people's data or disrupt the app.

4. The analysis is a guide

Tracking, team sorting and coach reports are made automatically and can be wrong: players can be missed, put in the wrong team or measured inaccurately. Always check the video before acting on a result. Don't use the results as the only basis for decisions about a person, such as selection, discipline or medical matters.

As far as the law allows, the owner is not liable for indirect damage or for decisions made using the results. Nothing in these terms limits liability that cannot be limited under Belgian law, such as for intent or gross negligence, and your rights as a consumer are not affected.
