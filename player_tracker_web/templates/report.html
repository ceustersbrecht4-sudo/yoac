04 Coach report

What the report flags, and the rule behind each flag

Pick a team and whether they were attacking or defending. Each flag comes with the moments it happened, a still with the problem marked in red, and a click takes the video there.

Without marking the pitch, distances are in body lengths: the height of a typical player in that frame.

work on

Holes in the defensive line

Two neighbouring defenders much further apart than they usually stand.

gap > 2.2 x usual spacing, and > usual + 1.5 body lengths
work on

Players out of line (dog-legs)

One defender standing well in front of or behind the rest of the line.

> 2.5 x the usual step out of line, at least 1.5 body lengths
work on

Outnumbered in defence / overlap chances

One side has more players standing in the line than the other.

2 or more extra players in the line
work on

Committing too many to the breakdown

Players who join the ruck aren't standing in the line.

defence: 3+ per ruck on average / attack: 4+
timed

Ruck speed

From the tackle until the ball is out, the way coaches time it, for every ruck the camera sees start to end. Elite teams get 60 to 70% of their rucks out in under 3 s. Scrums are counted too.

quick < 3 s / usable 3-6 s / slow > 6 s (elite: 60-70% quick) / scrum = 10+ players, 4+ per pack, not moving
work on

Possible offside at the ruck (pitch marked)

A defender standing in front of the offside line, the hindmost foot of the ruck on their side, just before the ball comes out.

> 1 m in front of the line, for most of the last 1 s before the ball is out (Law 15)
info

Blitz or drift (pitch marked)

After each ruck, whether the defending line came forward hard or slid out towards the touchline.

blitz: forward ≥ 2.5 m/s / drift: spreading out ≥ 1 m/s and faster than forward
work on

Backs too close at the scrum (pitch marked)

Backs must stay 5 m behind their own scrum's hindmost foot until it ends. The scrum-half may stand close.

< 4 m behind the hindmost foot, for most of the scrum (Law 19)
going well

The same checks, the other way round

A line that stayed connected, kept its shape or matched the attack's numbers is reported as going well.

These are measurements from one camera: a list of moments to rewatch, not a verdict. Check each one on the video.

Carries into contact, close-ups included

Team shape can't be judged from a close-up, but the ball carrier can, and a close-up shows them better. In the report for a team that's attacking, every carry into contact is checked, measured against the player's own size so zoom doesn't matter:

run-in

Running at space

In the last half second: heading for the gap between two defenders or a defender's edge, or straight at a chest. Plus footwork and speeding up or slowing down.

straight = within 12° of the defender / edge = 20° or more off / footwork = 20° change of direction
body

Body height and shoulder

From a pose model (shoulders, hips, knees, ankles) at the moment of contact: low or upright, and leading with the shoulder or going in square.

low = shoulders under 68% of standing height / upright = over 76%
after

Leg drive

Whether the carrier's feet keep going forward in the second after contact, or get pushed back.

drive = half a body length or more forward

Pick your tactics, check if they stuck

When you make a report for one team, tick the tactics you planned. For each one the report says in what share of the rucks the team played it, and whether it worked. A tactic you picked isn't flagged as a mistake: a gap you leave on purpose as a trap is checked as a trap, not as a hole.

Mark the pitch once, get metres

On a wide shot, click at least 4 spots where two pitch lines cross, like the 22 and the 5 m line. The app then follows the camera as it pans and zooms, and the report adds:

Line speed in the first 1.5 s after the ball leaves a ruck: under 1.5 m/s is flagged as passive, 2.5 m/s and more as good.
Mauls, told apart from rucks: a breakdown that moves 3 m or more.
Offside at the ruck: defenders in front of the hindmost foot just before the ball comes out.
Lineouts: both teams in a single line between the 5 m and 15 m lines, and whether a maul followed.

Following the camera runs once per video, a few minutes for a full match.

05 Soccer

Soccer: same tracking, its own report

Pick "Soccer" when you upload. Tracking, teams and the pitch marking work the same way. The report measures team shape in metres, so the pitch has to be marked: the corners of the boxes, where halfway meets a touchline, or the centre and penalty spots. Goalkeepers wear their own colour; the app finds them as the last player at each end and gives them back to their team. Eleven a side: anyone past that is a sub warming up or staff.

info

Where the block sits

How far the back line holds from its own goal without the ball.

low block ≤ 28 m / mid block 28-45 m / high line ≥ 45 m (FIFA line height)
work on

Stretched or compact

Back line to front line, and touchline side to side, without the ball.

compact: 25-35 m long, 30-35 m wide / stretched: over 40 m long / too wide: over 45 m
work on

Space between the lines

The gap between the back line, the midfield and the front players, where a player can turn.

8-12 m is compact / over 15 m for a quarter of the time is flagged
work on

Pressure on the ball (ball in view)

Whether the player on the ball has a defender close enough to stop them picking a pass.

pressure: a defender within 5 m / left alone: nobody within 8 m
info

The shape they play in

With all ten outfield players in view, the lines from back to front.

a new line starts 5 m further up / the shape seen most, at least 30% of the time
work on

Rest defence (ball in view)

How many stay behind the ball when attacking in the other half, ready for a lost ball.

fewer than 3 outfield players 10 m or more behind the ball is flagged / 4 or more is good

Soccer tactics you can pick

The same idea as rugby: tick what you planned, and the report says how much of the time the team played it and whether it worked. With the ball in view the report splits the video by which team had it; without, it uses your "attacking" or "defending" choice.
